About Me

OW✳

Olivia "Liv" Watson is from Memphis, Tennessee, pursuing an Honors BA in Film & Television at Columbia College Chicago, with minors in Public Relations and Immersive Media.

Her work spans producing, production design, and creative problem-solving, with experience in roles including Line Producer, Unit Production Manager, Art Director, and Art Assistant.

She is interested in the relationship between creative direction and the practical decisions that bring a project to life. Through film, design, and communications, she explores how thoughtful details can shape an audience's experience.
